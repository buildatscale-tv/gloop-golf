    How to play

    Putt

    Touch anywhere and drag back, away from where you want the ball to go. Let go to putt.

    A short pull is a soft putt. Pull farther for more power: near the end of the pull, the power grows faster. To stop, slide back to where you started and let go.

    The dotted path shows where the ball will roll, including the bounces.

    On a long pull, moving your finger sideways turns the aim only a little, so you can set far shots exactly. For a big turn, pull back less, turn, then pull again.

    To start the hole again from the tee, tap the round arrow at the top.

    The course

    Bounce off the wooden rails. Jelly bumpers kick the ball away. Sand slows it down, ice speeds it up, and hills pull it sideways.

    Water costs one stroke, and the ball goes back to where you putted from. Time your putt through the windmill when the sails are up.

    Special tiles

    Boost pads shoot the ball the way the arrows point. Conveyor belts carry it along. A portal sends the ball out of its twin, going the same way. Spinners and sliding walls bat it away, so time your putt.

    Trophies

    Win trophies for great shots, like a hole in one or a bank shot. Many trophies come from how the ball moves: bounce off walls, bumpers or another player's ball, slide on ice, ride a belt, or knock a friend's ball in. Some trophies unlock new ball styles. Pick a style for each player before the round.

    Score

    Each hole has a par: the number of putts a good player needs. Fewer strokes is better. A hole in one is the best!

    After 8 strokes, the ball is picked up and you score 8.

    Play together

    Up to 4 players take turns on one screen. Each player putts once, then the next player goes. All balls stay on the field where they stopped, and they hit each other. A ball knocked into the cup counts for its player with no extra stroke. A ball knocked into the water goes back with no penalty. When your ball drops, you are done with that hole. The scorecard shows who leads.

    To play the old way, turn off "Take turns" in Settings. Then each player putts until the ball drops.
